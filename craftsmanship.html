ARTISANAL JOURNEY

The Six Stages of Creation.

From architectural 3D drafting to final chronometric calibration, every step embodies horological rigor and unyielding standards.

01
01
STAGE 01 · DESIGN

Concept & Architectural Drafting

Initial hand sketches translated into 3D micro-mechanical CAD models to evaluate spatial geometry, balance spring oscillation, and weight distribution.

3D MICRO-CAD · SPATIAL TOLERANCE ±0.5µm
02
02
STAGE 02 · METALS

Noble Material Selection

Precious metal ingots cast and tested for structural integrity; diamonds sorted under 10x magnification for D-F color purity and VVS+ clarity.

18K CHAMPAGNE GOLD · VVS1 DIAMONDS
03
03
STAGE 03 · MACHINING

Precision CNC & Wire EDM Milling

Mainplates and movement bridges machined to sub-micron accuracy prior to hand finishing, ensuring gear pinions mesh with zero friction loss.

WIRE EDM MILLING · HIGH HOROLOGY GEARS
04
04
STAGE 04 · FINISHING

Hand Finishing & Anglage

Artisans apply gentian wood pegs and diamond paste to bevel every bridge edge to a mirror reflection, alongside Geneva stripes and Perlage graining.

HAND ANGLAGE · GENEVA STRIPES FINISH
05
05
STAGE 05 · HOROLOGY

Horological Assembly & Calibration

Master horologists adjust balance springs in 5 positions under chronometric observation to ensure accuracy exceeding official COSC certification limits.

5-POSITION TIMING · CHRONOMETRIC TEST
06
06
STAGE 06 · PRESENTATION

Final Inspection & Presentation

Timepieces placed in hand-carved mahogany presentation boxes accompanied by original horological bulletin de marche and certificate of origin.

BULLETIN DE MARCHE · LIFELONG WARRANTY
DOCUMENTARY ATELIER

Behind the Workbench.

In our Zurich workshop, quiet concentration reigns. A watchmaker spends up to three weeks on a single flying tourbillon cage, balancing components weighing less than half a gram.

This patience ensures that every movement operates with fluid harmony, producing a tactile resistance and acoustic resonance that machine assembly simply cannot replicate.
