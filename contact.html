Frequently Asked Questions.

How do bespoke commissions work?

+

Bespoke commissions begin with a consultation to explore initial design concepts, metal preferences, and gemstone selection. Following approval of 3D renders, our master craftsmen bring the piece to life with real-time updates accessible via our Workflow Manager portal.

Can an existing collection design be personalized?

+

Yes. All AURELLE timepieces and fine jewelry items can be customized with hand-engraved monograms, specific gemstone selections, custom leather straps, or distinct gold alloy finishes.

How can I request a private consultation?

+

Simply complete our online inquiry form or email concierge@aurelle-atelier.com. Our concierge will contact you within 24 hours to arrange an in-person salon visit or virtual consultation.

Can I follow my commission progress?

+

Absolutely. Every client receives a secure project reference code allowing them to monitor milestone stages, view workshop photo updates, and review quality inspection reports in our Workflow Manager.

How are project updates shared?

+

Updates are published directly to your client workflow portal as key milestones are completed—such as CAD approval, metal casting, stone setting, and chronometric timing tests.
